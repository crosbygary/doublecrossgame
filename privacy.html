YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Racing Go 0.4.1

Racing Go works offline. The app does not send your gameplay or personal information to the developer.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the Racing Go iPhone app and its support pages. The app developer can be contacted at cr0sby_gar@icloud.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your fictional cash balance, unlocked and selected cars, best payout, run count, active run and route choices, audio preferences and haptic setting in a local save file. This information is used to restore your progress and settings. No account is required.

The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, developer-operated cloud storage, social features or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you include. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by the email providers involved, including iCloud Mail for the support inbox. Please do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

Sharing

The developer does not sell your information or use it for advertising. Support correspondence is processed by email providers to deliver and store messages, and may be disclosed if required by applicable law. In-game save data is not transmitted to the developer.

Retention and your choices

Your local progress remains on the device until the app and its data are deleted. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local save. The operating system may include app data in device backups; you can manage those backups through Apple or your backup provider. Reinstalling or restoring a backup may restore saved progress.

Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve requests and maintain a record of the response, or to meet applicable legal requirements. You can ask to access, correct or delete information you sent to support by emailing cr0sby_gar@icloud.com. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location. There is no in-app tracking consent to withdraw because the app does not track you.

Apple services and diagnostics

Apple may process App Store downloads, device backups and diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. These platform services are separate from the app's local gameplay storage.

These support pages

The copies bundled inside the app load locally and do not contact a website. The pages contain no analytics, cookies, forms or third-party fonts. When you visit a copy hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Choosing an email link opens your email app; it does not send a message automatically.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request its deletion.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy available through Settings.
