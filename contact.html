Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot or the steps that reproduce the issue can help. Please avoid including private information.

Where is my progress?

Progress is saved on the device. Pausing and choosing Save & Main Menu keeps the current run. Cash out at a checkpoint to add your run balance to the garage. Deleting the app can remove local progress.

Can I withdraw in-game cash?

No. All currency is fictional and only unlocks cars in the game. There are no real-money purchases, payouts or prizes.
